# Attribution and provenance

## Academic work

These university coursework archives include group submissions. Descriptions cover the submitted implementations and recorded experiments, not an independently verified allocation of individual contributions. Personal names, usernames, student identifiers and course-repository links have been removed. Original submissions remain outside this repository.

## Models, data and evidence

Library/model identifiers describe dependencies, not ownership. Project `DATA.md` files list required external inputs. No dataset or pretrained-weight redistribution permission is granted by this repository.

Retained plots are selected source figures without identifying content or explicitly labeled plots reconstructed from recorded aggregates. No new training achievements were created. Whole reports, source-image examples and clinical screenshots with uncertain publication status are not redistributed.

No new repository-wide license is applied to the coursework. Applicable course/group publication permissions still need confirmation before publication; ZIP contents alone do not establish them.
